# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

RankPilot AI is an AI-powered SaaS platform for:
- SEO optimization
- AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
- AI content generation
- Keyword research
- Competitor analysis
- Website audit
- AI visibility tracking (Google + ChatGPT + Gemini + Perplexity)

Goal:
Build a modern SaaS that helps websites rank in both:
- Traditional search engines (Google, Bing)
- AI search engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, AI Overviews)
